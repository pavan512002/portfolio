Privacy Policy

Calendar Slot Finder — Chrome extension. Last updated: September 2026.

What this extension does

Calendar Slot Finder helps you find, share, and convert available time slots using your Google Calendar. It runs entirely in your browser as a Chrome extension.

Data we access

  • Google Calendar data (via OAuth scopes calendar.readonly and calendar.freebusy): your calendar events and free/busy information, used only to compute available time slots you request. Teammates' free/busy data is read only when you explicitly add them and they have shared their calendar with you.
  • Google account email: used solely to check your Pro subscription status.

Data we store

  • On your device (chrome.storage): your settings (working hours, timezone, contacts you add) and a cached subscription check. Nothing else.
  • On our server (Supabase): your email, subscription status, and subscription expiry date — only if you purchase Pro. This is the minimum needed to verify your subscription.

Payments

Pro subscriptions are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record. We never see, handle, or store your card details — all payment information goes directly to Lemon Squeezy under their privacy policy.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing.
  • We never read your email, contacts, or any Google data beyond Calendar free/busy scopes.
  • We never use your calendar data for advertising or profiling.

Data retention & deletion

Settings stay on your device until you remove the extension or clear them. Subscription records are kept while your subscription is active (plus a short grace period) and deleted on request.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, reach out via GitHub.
