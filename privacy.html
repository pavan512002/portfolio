Privacy Policy

freeslot — Chrome extension. Last updated: November 2026.

What this extension does

freeslot helps you find, share, and convert available time slots using your Google Calendar. The calendar features run entirely in your browser as a Chrome extension.

Data we access

  • Google Calendar data, through these OAuth scopes: calendar.events.readonly (your events), calendar.calendarlist.readonly (the list of your calendars, so you can choose which count as busy) and calendar.freebusy (free/busy information). It is used only to compute the available time slots you ask for. Teammates' free/busy data is read only when you add them and they have shared their calendar with you.
  • Google account email, through the separate userinfo.email scope. It is requested only when you subscribe to Pro or check your subscription, and it is used solely to link and verify that subscription.

Calendar data goes only between your browser and Google. It is never sent to our server or to any other third party. The email token used for billing is a separate token that carries no calendar access.

Data we store

  • On your device (chrome.storage): your settings (working hours, timezone, contacts you add), your selected calendars, and a cached subscription check. Nothing else.
  • On our server: we run a small billing function (hosted on Supabase) with no database. It checks your Google token with Google, then passes your Google account ID and email to Polar to start a checkout or look up your subscription. We do not keep a copy of your email or subscription records.
  • At Polar: your Google account ID, email and subscription records are stored by Polar as part of processing your subscription.

Payments

Pro subscriptions are processed by Polar, our merchant of record, under Polar's privacy policy. We never see, handle, or store your card details; they go directly to Polar and its payment processor.

Google API Services User Data Policy

freeslot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing.
  • We never read your email, contacts, or any Google data beyond the scopes listed above.
  • We never use your calendar data for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.
  • We never let people read your data except where needed to provide the features you use.

Data retention & deletion

Settings and cached data stay on your device until you remove the extension or clear them. You can revoke freeslot's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have your subscription and customer record at Polar deleted, email the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is used, the date at the top will be updated and, for material changes, the extension's store listing will say so.
