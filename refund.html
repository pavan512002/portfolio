Free trial

Every Pro plan starts with a 7-day free trial. You are not charged during the trial. Cancel before it ends and you will not be charged at all.

Refunds

Because every plan starts with a free trial, you can try Pro before you pay, and payments are non-refundable once charged. Exceptions:

  • Duplicate or mistaken charges: if you were charged twice or charged in error, I will refund the extra charge in full.
  • Service problems: if Pro features were broken or unavailable for a significant part of a billing period, I will refund the affected time.
  • Where the law requires it: if consumer law where you live gives you a right to a refund, that right is not affected.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from the customer portal (in the extension: Settings → Plan → Manage subscription). Cancelling stops future renewals; Pro stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. Cancel before the trial ends, or before a renewal date, to avoid the next charge.
