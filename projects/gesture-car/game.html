Dodge Run

You survived the demo — now survive the road. Barriers rain from the top and get faster every second. Steer with the same hand gestures, your arrow keys, or the touch buttons. Your score is how many seconds you last.

🤟 3 fingers = left · 🖖 4 = right · ✊ fist = stop

How to play

🤟 Steer with gestures

Enable the camera and hold up 3 fingers to swerve left, 4 fingers to swerve right. Make a fist to hold position. Same finger-counting as the robot car.

⌨️ Or use keys / touch

No camera? ← → or A / D work fine, and on phones you get big ◀ ▶ buttons. Press Space to start or restart.

🏆 Scoring

Every second survived counts. Barriers fall faster and more often the longer you last. Your best time is saved on this device — beat it.
