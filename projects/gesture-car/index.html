Gesture-Controlled Robot Car

Hold up fingers, and a car moves. A webcam tracks your hand with MediaPipe, counts your fingers, and steers a robot car wirelessly over Bluetooth. Try it below — your camera becomes the controller.

How the real thing works

1 · See the hand hand_module.py

MediaPipe finds 21 hand landmarks in each webcam frame. A finger counts as "up" when its tip sits above its knuckle (thumb: tip past the joint, sideways).

2 · Count → command final.py

The finger count (0–5) is sent as a single character over USB serial to the sender Arduino at 38400 baud.

3 · Go wireless sender.ino

The sender Arduino relays each character over Bluetooth (HC-05) to the car — no wires between you and the robot.

4 · Drive car.ino

The car's Arduino maps the character to motor directions on an H-bridge driver: forward, backward, left, right, stop.
