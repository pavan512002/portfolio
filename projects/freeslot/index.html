Calendar Slot Finder

"When are you free?" — answered in one click. A Chrome extension that reads your Google Calendar and finds, shares, and converts available time slots across timezones.

JavaScriptChrome MV3Google OAuthSupabaseLemon Squeezy

Four modes

🎯 Solo

Find your own free time across a date range, inside working hours, in your timezone. One-click copy per slot or everything at once.

👥 Team ● Pro

Add teammates by email and find overlapping availability across everyone's calendars. Missing calendar access is reported, never guessed.

🖱️ Select ● Pro

A full-page visual week calendar. Drag across days to hand-pick time blocks, see your events and teammates' busy times overlaid, resize and move blocks freely.

🌍 Time Convert free forever

Convert any date/time across 400+ timezones with live UTC offsets, working-hours warnings, and per-row copy.
